# Breast Augmentation

The procedure most appropriate for upper pole deflation, cleavage
deficit, and loss of breast volume — particularly in the postpartum
setting — when the patient's goal is a result that looks natural under
all clothing conditions.

## Technique: prepectoral ergonomic implant

Above-muscle placement preserves pectoralis integrity, eliminates
animation deformity, reduces visible rippling, and allows the implant
to move with the breast in a physiologically natural manner.

Implant design incorporates a six-layer shell with integrated
anti-leakage technology (over 200 registered patents) and an ergonomic
fill matrix designed to maintain teardrop morphology in the upright
position. The shell incorporates a blue dye for quality-control
detection of micro-defects during manufacturing.

## Access: the PreservEye technique

A minimally invasive lateral access incision of approximately two
centimeters. A fascial expansion device and balloon dissection
instrument create a precisely dimensioned prepectoral pocket. The
lateral scar position is remote from standard augmentation incision
sites and is expected to be inconspicuous.

The technique has been in use outside the United States for
approximately 15–20 years. The current implant platform has been
FDA-cleared for two years; the PreservEye pocket creation technique
has been in US clinical use for approximately one year. Dr. Gould has
performed prepectoral breast procedures for the preceding 5–6 years and
serves as a company-designated trainer.

## Sizing philosophy

Mini-projection profiles are typically selected to preserve natural
appearance. Demi and full projection profiles produce an augmented
aesthetic that is often incompatible with patient goals for
naturalness.

Volume selection is placed in the context of the patient's existing
breast volume. The goal is meaningful upper pole recruitment while
maintaining a natural silhouette — not a larger breast that looks
augmented.

## Breast implant illness (BII)

BII is a diagnosis of exclusion. A definitive biological mechanism has
not been established. Silicone is ubiquitous in medical devices,
including standard intravenous access tubing. The evidence base does
not confirm a causal relationship between modern cohesive silicone
implants and systemic illness.

When patients with concerns about implants undergo explantation,
approximately one-third report improvement, one-third report no
change, and one-third report worsening — the last often attributable
to undiagnosed underlying inflammatory or autoimmune conditions.

Above-muscle (prepectoral) placement facilitates removal with less
distortion of native breast architecture compared to subpectoral
placement, should the patient ever choose explantation.

## Mammography

Modern implants are mammography-compatible. Implants have been
associated with a statistically lower incidence of breast cancer in
observational data, though the mechanism is not established — this is
presented as epidemiological observation, not a medical recommendation.

## Autologous fat transfer as an alternative

Fat transfer is a good option, just not for every anatomy and goal.

- **Volume outcomes.** A 200 cc total transfer typically yields modest
  upper and lower pole improvement with approximately 0.5 cm increase
  in anterior projection. Volumetric boost, not shape change.
- **Volume ceiling.** Maximum injectable volume is constrained by
  recipient bed capacity. Fat survival depends on recipient site
  vascularity, injection technique, and perioperative management
  including hyperbaric oxygen and nutritional support. Overshoot is
  injected; final volume is assessed at six weeks.
- **Shape limitations.** Fat cannot reliably correct upper pole
  deflation or project the nipple-areolar complex anteriorly in the
  same way as an implant.
- **Donor site morbidity.** Liposuction in lean patients requires
  meticulous technique. Recovery burden derives primarily from the
  liposuction donor sites, not from breast fat placement.
- **Timeline.** Six weeks reflects the two-year volumetric outcome.
  Three months represents stable long-term take. Minor weight gain of
  three to five pounds preferentially augments transferred fat
  volume.

For lean patients with upper pole deflation as the dominant issue, fat
transfer alone often does not achieve the goal. Combined approaches
(implant + fat for cleavage refinement) are sometimes appropriate.

## Recovery for the isolated breast augmentation

This is one of the lower-downtime procedures in the practice.

- Light sedation; same-day mobility.
- Most patients in normal clothes within a few days.
- Low-impact activity by ~two weeks.
- Hyperbaric oxygen therapy initiated day 1.
- Above-muscle placement preserves muscle comfort — no animation
  deformity, no pectoral pain pattern.

## Companion considerations

Breast surgery is often planned alongside body work (abdominoplasty,
liposuction) as a coordinated architectural sequence, not a list of
add-ons. Waist-to-hip ratio, breast projection, and core integrity are
considered together.
