# Consultation Process

A consultation at Gould Plastic Surgery is the starting point for any
procedural conversation. It is the only setting in which specific
recommendations, candidacy decisions, and pricing are discussed.

## What a consultation includes

- **60 to 90 minutes** with Dr. Gould.
- Review of goals, medical history, prior surgeries, current medications
  and supplements, allergies, and any prior aesthetic procedures.
- Focused physical examination relevant to the area of interest.
- Discussion of realistic options. Surgical and non-surgical
  recommendations are both possible outcomes; not every patient is
  steered toward surgery.
- Standardized photographic documentation.
- Imaging review when applicable (e.g., a CT scan brought from a prior
  evaluation).
- Surgical and recovery logistics: anesthesia, OR location, expected
  timeline, follow-up cadence.
- Pricing discussion specific to the recommended plan. Pricing is
  provided in person after examination, never in advance over DM, email,
  or phone.

## What to bring

- A government-issued photo ID.
- A current medication list, including supplements.
- Any prior operative reports, imaging, or pathology relevant to the
  consult.
- Photo references if helpful — patients sometimes find it easier to
  show rather than describe what they like or dislike.
- Questions written down. Patients are encouraged to ask everything.

## Virtual consults

Available for out-of-area patients. A virtual visit follows the same
structure, with examination limited to what photos and video can convey.
An in-person visit is still required before any surgical decision is
finalized.

## After the consult

- A written summary is sent. It includes the surgical or non-surgical
  recommendation, the proposed plan, and pricing for the recommended
  plan.
- Scheduling, deposit, pre-op visit, and surgery date are coordinated by
  the surgical scheduler.
- Follow-up is included for the post-op period and beyond at no
  additional cost.

## What we do not say in DM

A specific recommendation, candidacy decision, or price for an
individual patient before a consultation. Those are surgical decisions
that require an examination by Dr. Gould.
