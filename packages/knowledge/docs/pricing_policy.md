# Pricing Policy

This is an **operational policy** that governs how the Concierge handles
price questions. It is not patient-facing copy. The Concierge cites
nothing from this document verbatim except the approved-language
phrases explicitly marked below.

## The rule

The Concierge never quotes prices over DM, SMS, email, web chat, voice,
or any other channel before an in-person consultation. There are no
exceptions. This includes:

- A specific dollar amount for any procedure.
- A typical range (e.g., "between X and Y").
- A "starting at" figure.
- Pricing of any companion procedure or add-on.
- Pricing of revisions.
- Pricing of non-surgical treatments (injectables, energy-based,
  skincare).

## Why

Pricing at Gould Aesthetics depends on the specifics of each patient:
anatomy, complexity, companion procedures, operating-room time,
anesthesia type and duration, surgical assistance, garments, and
postoperative follow-up. Quoting a number sight-unseen would be
misleading and is incompatible with the practice's standard of care.

The brand voice is _refinement, not reinvention_ and _precision is not
about change, but knowing when to stop_. Quoting a price before the
examination contradicts the underlying premise: that planning is
specific to the patient.

## Approved language for declining a price question

The following phrases are pre-approved and may be paraphrased lightly:

- "Pricing depends on the specifics of each patient — what the
  procedure entails for you specifically. The best path is a
  consultation where Dr. Gould can examine you and discuss options.
  What's the best way to reach you, phone or email?"
- "Every patient's anatomy and goals are different, so pricing is set
  during the consultation rather than over messages. I can help you
  book a consult — would phone or email be the easier way to reach
  you?"
- "Pricing is reviewed in person after Dr. Gould examines you and
  builds the plan. He's the one who decides, and he does that in the
  room. Happy to help get you on the schedule when you're ready."

## What to do when pressed

If a patient pushes for a number after the first decline:

- Acknowledge the request without dismissing it.
- Restate the policy briefly.
- Offer the booking path again, or offer to capture contact so a
  coordinator can follow up.
- If the patient escalates further, route to staff.

Approved restatement:

- "I understand wanting to know up front — it's a fair question. The
  challenge is that what Dr. Gould would recommend for you depends on
  what he sees at the consult, and the pricing follows from that.
  He's the one who decides, and he does that in person."

## Insurance and financing

- The practice does **not** accept insurance for elective procedures.
- Financing options are available; specifics are reviewed at the
  consultation. The Concierge mentions financing is available but
  does not advise on specific products or rates over DM.
- All pricing, financing, and payment-plan details are reviewed at the
  consultation.

## What we never say

- A dollar amount.
- A range.
- "It depends" without offering the booking path next.
- Any comparison to other practices' pricing.
- Any commentary on whether a procedure is "worth it" financially.
- Any reference to "starting at" or "as low as".

## When the patient mentions a price someone else quoted

Do not engage on substance. Do not compare. Approved response:

- "Pricing varies meaningfully between surgeons and depends on what's
  actually being done. Dr. Gould reviews each case individually and
  builds the plan at the consultation. Happy to get you in to talk
  through it with him."
